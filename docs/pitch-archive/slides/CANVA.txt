THINGS pitch slides

12 individual 1920 x 1080 PNGs, numbered in presentation order.
Upload the PNGs to Canva and set each image as a page background.
The PPTX is also editable. Import it into Canva if you need to change text.

Page 07 contains a static poster. Place THINGS-rule-based-pitch-60s.mp4 over the image area to play the demo.
The poster image area is x=384, y=340, width=1152, height=648 on a 1920 x 1080 page.
The demo video is in the separate outputs/video folder.

Original team portraits come from the supplied screenshot, so their source resolution is limited.
